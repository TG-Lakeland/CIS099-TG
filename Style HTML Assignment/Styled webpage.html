About

D&D is a turn based fantasy board game that utilizes dice to calculate actions, damage and even character stats. The DM, or "Dungeon Master" is in control of the story and writes adventures for the players to embark on. The players must create a character to their liking and work together to survive the campaign because if your character dies then that's the end of their story.

Details

What you need to play.

  • Dice are used to calculate damage and actions your charater does. Dice included:D-20, D-12, D-10, D-8, D-6, and D-4
  • Character Sheet with your characters stats and backstory. Your stats are important in calculating how affective your character is at certain actions as well as their race, class, and proficiencies.
  • Pen and Paper is vital for D&D. No one likes math but having a sheet to write down your health and how much damage your character takes. Your character will be hurt. A lot.

Featured Highlight

In your party's adventure you'll encounter your fair share of close calls with death, but with teamwork and a good strategy you can accomplish a lot. The main advice I'd give to new players is to have fun. The main goal isn't to win but to get to the end of the story, whether thats with the character you started with or one of many you've created.
