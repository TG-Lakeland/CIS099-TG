Halo: A Franchise That Defined A Generation

Halo is a game series set in the year 2552 in a future where humanity is at conflict with an alien faction known as the covenant. You play as a Spartan named Master Chief. He is humanity's last hope at stopping the covenant from activating a universe ending weapon known as "Halo".

Amazing Story Full of Lore

The Halo universe has an abundance of world building to keep people captivated and makes it feel real. From Super soldiers with a tragic backstory to a cosmic horror hivemind trying to conquer all knowledge in the universe. There is so much love and appreciation for this franchise.

The Complete Halo Saga In 22 Minutes

Great Soundtrack

Halo has one of the most iconic soundtracks in all of gaming. If you were to play the first ten seconds of the song to a complete stranger on the street chances are they have heard this theme before. The angelic choir gives us an almost tanquil vibe into the unknown and has stuck with people for decades.
